About us

Your neighbor with the right tools

Help Around The House is a local handyman service for Fargo homeowners who'd rather spend their weekends on something other than repairs.

Our story

Local help for everyday jobs

Every house has a list. The faucet that drips, the closet door that won't latch, the shelf that's been leaning against the wall since you moved in. Those jobs are too small for a contractor but too annoying to keep putting off.

That's where we come in. We're based right here in Fargo and we take on the jobs other companies don't want, done carefully and at a fair price.

How we work

What you can count on

Upfront pricing

You get the price before work starts, and the bill matches it.

On time

We show up when we say we will, and we keep you posted if anything changes.

Clean job sites

We cover floors, clean up dust and take the debris with us.

No job too small

One loose hinge or a whole list, we're glad to help either way.
